Los Angeles County

Drug & Alcohol Rehab Serving Los Angeles, California

For people across Los Angeles County looking for help with drug or alcohol use, finding the right level of care in a city this large can feel overwhelming. California Treatment Centers serves Los Angeles and the surrounding area, connecting residents from Downtown and Hollywood to the San Fernando Valley, the Westside and the South Bay with evidence-based addiction treatment. We help LA residents access our California programs and, when it helps recovery, arrange placement and travel to one of our other locations across the state.

Addiction Treatment for Los Angeles Residents

Los Angeles is enormous and so are the pressures that can fuel substance use — long commutes on the 101 and 405, high cost of living, a demanding nightlife and entertainment culture, and the isolation that can come with all of it. Wherever you are in the county, from Pasadena-adjacent neighborhoods to Long Beach, care that meets you where you are matters. We aim to make the first call simple and judgment-free.

Levels of Care We Offer

Addiction recovery usually works best as a continuum, stepping down as you stabilize. We help Los Angeles-area clients access the full range:

  • Medical detox — medically supervised withdrawal management for alcohol, opioids and other substances.
  • Residential treatment — 24/7 structured care in a live-in setting.
  • Partial hospitalization (PHP) — full-day clinical programming while living off-site or in sober housing.
  • Intensive outpatient (IOP) and general outpatient — flexible schedules that fit work, school or family in LA.
  • Sober living and sober companionship — supportive housing and one-on-one recovery support.

What We Treat

We work with adults facing a range of substance use challenges, including alcohol, opioids and fentanyl, methamphetamine, cocaine, benzodiazepines and prescription medications. Many people also live with co-occurring conditions such as anxiety, depression or trauma, and our programs are designed to address mental health alongside substance use.

Alcohol

Structured support for alcohol dependence, including medically supervised detox when needed.

Opioids & Fentanyl

Withdrawal management and medication-assisted treatment options.

Stimulants

Care for methamphetamine and cocaine use, common across LA County.

Co-Occurring

Integrated treatment for mental health and substance use together.

Insurance & Cost

We are in-network with most major insurers, and we offer free, confidential benefit verification so you understand your coverage before committing to anything. Many Los Angeles residents are surprised by how much of treatment their plan covers. You can verify your insurance online or by phone.

What to Expect When You Reach Out

\n

Your first call is a confidential conversation, not a commitment. Our admissions team listens to what is happening, asks about your history and goals, and helps you understand which level of care fits. From there we coordinate your verification of benefits, scheduling and, when residential or detox is the right starting point, a smooth hand-off into care across the Los Angeles area.

\n

Personalized, Evidence-Based Treatment

\n

No two people arrive at recovery the same way. Plans are built around your needs and may include individual therapy, group sessions, family involvement, relapse-prevention skills and, where appropriate, medication-assisted treatment overseen by a physician. Approaches such as cognitive behavioral therapy and motivational interviewing are core to how we work.

\n

\n

We focus on removing friction — clear answers, fast insurance checks and warm coordination — so you can spend your energy on getting well rather than navigating a confusing system. Recovery is possible, and you do not have to do it alone.
